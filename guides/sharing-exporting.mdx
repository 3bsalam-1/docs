The fastest way to make a conversation useful is to share it. BRIEF gives you four export paths — WhatsApp, PDF, DOCX, and clipboard — each suited to a different context and recipient. The محضر BRIEF generates is structured, bilingual, and professionally formatted, so in most cases you can share it immediately after the meeting without any editing. This guide explains every sharing option, what gets included, and what to consider before you send.

## WhatsApp Share

Tap **Share → WhatsApp** to send a WhatsApp-ready محضر — a clean, formatted summary that includes decisions and action items, laid out for instant readability in a chat window.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Share">
    Open the conversation output and tap the Share icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select WhatsApp">
    Choose WhatsApp from the share sheet. BRIEF formats the محضر specifically for WhatsApp — structured text with clear section headers, bold labels, and bullet points that render correctly in both iOS and Android WhatsApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a chat or group">
    WhatsApp opens with the محضر pre-loaded in the message field. Select the contact, group, or channel you want to send it to, and tap Send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The WhatsApp محضر is designed to be sent to clients immediately after a meeting — many BRIEF users do this before they've left the room. The recipient doesn't need to have BRIEF installed or even know what BRIEF is. They receive a clean, professional summary that reads as if you wrote it yourself.
</Tip>

## PDF Export

The PDF export produces a full, bilingual document ready for professional distribution or filing.

The PDF includes:

- **Cover information**: conversation title, date, participants, duration, and conversation type
- **Arabic محضر**: right-to-left layout with correct Arabic font rendering and typography
- **English action items**: owners and deadlines in a clean table
- All other output sections: key points, decisions, risks, requirements, commitments, and follow-ups

<Accordion title="Typography note">
  BRIEF's PDF renderer uses a professional Arabic typeface with full right-to-left layout. Arabic text is never displayed left-to-right or in a degraded font — the PDF looks the same on any device, regardless of whether the recipient has Arabic fonts installed.
</Accordion>

To export a PDF:

1. Tap **Share → Export as PDF**
2. BRIEF generates the document (typically under 10 seconds)
3. Save to Files, share to email, or send via any app

## DOCX Export

The DOCX export produces an editable Word document with the same content as the PDF.

Use DOCX when:

- The recipient needs to **add to or annotate** the output
- The document will be incorporated into **a larger report or proposal**
- Your organisation's workflow requires **an editable file format**

<Note>
  DOCX files preserve Arabic text and right-to-left formatting when opened in Microsoft Word or Google Docs with Arabic language support enabled. If the recipient opens the file in an environment without Arabic font support, text may not render correctly. For guaranteed rendering, share the PDF instead.
</Note>

To export a DOCX:

1. Tap **Share → Export as Word Document**
2. BRIEF generates the file
3. Save to Files or share directly

## Copy to Clipboard

Tap **Share → Copy to Clipboard** to copy the full text output of the conversation.

The clipboard copy includes all sections — summary, key points, decisions, action items, and the rest — as plain text. Paste it into an email, a Notion page, a Slack message, or any other tool in your workflow.

<Tip>
  The clipboard copy is the fastest path to tools like Notion, Confluence, or Linear. Paste the output and restructure it however you need.
</Tip>

## Bilingual Output

By default, BRIEF generates:

- An **Arabic محضر** — a formal Arabic-language record of the meeting, in the register and format of a professional محضر اجتماع
- **English action items** — extracted tasks with owners and deadlines, in English

The language balance adapts to the conversation. A meeting conducted entirely in Arabic produces a fully Arabic output. A code-switched meeting — where participants move between Arabic and English — produces output that reflects that naturally.

To change the default language preference, go to **Settings → Output Language**.

## What Is and Isn't Shareable

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Always shareable" icon="check" color="#16a34a">
    **Derived outputs** — summaries, decisions, action items, key points, risks, and all other extracted fields — can always be shared. These are your notes about your meeting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Requires consent check" icon="triangle-exclamation" color="#d97706">
    **Audio recordings** are subject to separate consent and permission settings. Whether you can share audio depends on your processing mode and what consent was obtained before recording.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Sharing the notes BRIEF produces from a conversation is different from sharing a recording of that conversation. The محضر and structured outputs are your professional record of what was agreed — you own them and can share them freely. Sharing the audio of a conversation involving other people requires their consent. Always review your consent settings before sharing audio. See **Settings → Recording & Consent** for details.
</Note>

In **Mode A** (on-device processing), the audio file never leaves your device. You can share the derived outputs freely, but the audio file is local only and is not included in any BRIEF share flow.
