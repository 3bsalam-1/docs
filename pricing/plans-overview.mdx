BRIEF's pricing is built around two dimensions: **Consumption** (how much cloud AI processing and storage you use) and **Capability** (what features, conversation types, and workspace tools you can access). Understanding both dimensions helps you choose the plan that matches how you actually work — and avoid paying for capacity you don't need.

## Two Pricing Dimensions

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Consumption" icon="gauge">
    **Cloud AI hours** — the processing time BRIEF uses when transcribing and analyzing your conversations in Mode B or Mode C (cloud-assisted modes).

    Also covers **storage** (how long your conversation history is retained in the cloud) and **memory depth** (how far back BRIEF's AI can reach when generating summaries or answering questions).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Capability" icon="sparkles">
    **Features you can unlock** — conversation types (Professional and Generic), Speaker Labels, team workspace, admin controls, and the depth of AI-generated outputs available per conversation.

    Capability tiers gate what BRIEF can *do* with your data, not just how much of it it processes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The On-Device Principle

<Note>
  On-device processing (Mode A) is **always unlimited at every tier**, including Free. It costs BRIEF nothing to run on your device, so it is never metered, never capped, and never gated behind a paywall. You can record, transcribe, and get basic outputs offline at any tier, forever.
</Note>

## Soft Caps: You're Never Blocked

When you reach your monthly cloud AI hour limit, BRIEF **degrades gracefully to on-device processing** — it does not lock your notes or stop your session. You still get output; it may process a little slower or with slightly reduced accuracy depending on your device. Your notes are never held hostage.

<Tip>
  **The three natural upgrade moments:**
  - **Free → Starter:** You'll notice a meaningful jump in dialect accuracy and cloud transcription quality. If you're recording in Egyptian Arabic or Gulf Arabic — or mixing Arabic and English — Starter's cloud processing handles them noticeably better.
  - **Starter → Pro:** Full conversation archive and deeper AI outputs unlock — search across your entire history with provenance, and get richer structured outputs from every Professional conversation.
  - **Pro → Business:** Team sharing and multi-seat pooling make sense when your team is all using BRIEF and you want shared context, pooled cloud hours, and roster-level Speaker Labels across your organisation.
</Tip>

## Regional Pricing

BRIEF maintains **two separate regional price sheets** — Egypt and GCC (KSA/UAE). These are structural price tiers, not discount codes or promotional rates. The same plan genuinely costs a different amount depending on your region, reflecting local purchasing power and market conditions.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Egypt" icon="map-pin">
    Lower price points designed for the Egyptian market. Your region is detected at signup, but you can confirm it in your account settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GCC (KSA / UAE)" icon="map-pin">
    Standard GCC pricing for Saudi Arabia, UAE, and neighboring Gulf markets. Reflects the purchasing environment and local payment infrastructure in those markets.

## Payment Options

Choose how you pay based on what's convenient for you:

<Steps>
  <Step title="App Store / Google Play (IAP)">
    Subscribe directly through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Billing is handled by Apple or Google, and you can manage or cancel your subscription from your device settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Local Payment Rails (Egypt)">
    Pay using **Fawry**, **Paymob**, or **Vodafone Cash** — no international card required. Select your preferred local method at checkout in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Web Checkout">
    Where available, subscribe through BRIEF's web checkout for direct billing. Check the app for availability in your region.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What counts as a 'cloud AI hour'?">
    A cloud AI hour is the processing time BRIEF uses on its servers when it transcribes and analyzes your conversation in **Mode B** (hybrid) or **Mode C** (full cloud) processing. It covers the compute time for transcription, speaker detection, and AI-generated outputs like summaries, action items, and memory indexing.

    On-device processing (Mode A) does **not** count against your cloud hours — it runs entirely on your device and is never metered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I hit my cloud hour limit?">
    BRIEF **degrades gracefully to on-device processing** — it does not stop your session or lock your existing notes. You keep recording and generating outputs; the AI just runs locally on your device instead of in the cloud. Depending on your device, this may be slightly slower or produce slightly different output quality, but you are never blocked from using BRIEF.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch plans at any time?">
    Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period so you keep access to your current plan's features until then.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are prices different for Egypt vs GCC?">
    Yes — this is a structural difference, not a promotional discount. BRIEF maintains two separate price sheets: one for Egypt, and one for GCC (Saudi Arabia and UAE). Your region is determined at signup and reflected in the prices you see in-app. These are genuine regional tiers designed for different market contexts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I try Pro features before paying?">
    The Free tier gives you a real taste of BRIEF's cloud quality with ~3–5 cloud AI hours per month. This is enough to experience accurate dialect transcription and AI-generated structured outputs. The full conversation archive and deeper Professional outputs require a Pro subscription, but your Free-tier conversations are still saved and become searchable when you upgrade.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
