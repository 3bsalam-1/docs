The BRIEF Education plan exists because students use BRIEF differently from professionals. A working professional records a handful of meetings per week; a full-time student records three to ten lectures, every week, for four months straight. The Education plan matches that rhythm — semester-based pricing, lecture-specific outputs, course memory that spans an entire term, and a summer pause that keeps your data safe without charging you when you're not in class.

<Note>
  The Education plan is for **adults (18+) enrolled at a university or higher-education institution**. BRIEF does not serve school-age learners. If you are under 18, BRIEF is not the right tool for you.
</Note>

## What's Included

Every Education plan subscription gives you access to BRIEF's full suite of lecture-specific outputs and study tools:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lecture-Type Outputs">
    BRIEF generates outputs built for academic content: **structured summary**, **key concepts**, **definitions**, **Q&A from the lecture**, and a **learning gaps analysis** that highlights what the lecturer emphasized versus what you may have missed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flashcards and Quiz">
    Turn any lecture into a revision deck instantly. Every flashcard and quiz question links back to the **exact timestamp** in the recording where that concept was covered. You can tap any card and jump straight to the moment in the lecture it came from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Tutor (Grounded in Your Transcript)">
    Ask the AI tutor questions about the lecture — and get answers sourced from *your recording*, not from the internet. Every response the tutor gives is grounded in your actual lecture transcript, so you know it reflects what your lecturer actually said, not a generic explanation from somewhere else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Course Memory">
    Organize your lectures by course. BRIEF's course memory lets you search and ask questions **across all the lectures in a course** — so a week before your exam, you can ask "what did the lecturer say about market equilibrium across all sessions?" and get a synthesized answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revision Mode">
    Before exams, activate Revision Mode. BRIEF surfaces the concepts, gaps, and questions that matter most based on what you've recorded across the term. It's a study session built from your actual lectures, not a generic topic list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Processing: Mode A/B by Default

<Tip>
  **Best practice for lecture recording:** Record in **Mode A** (on-device, fully offline) during the lecture itself — no internet connection needed, and nothing is sent anywhere while you're in the room. When you get back to Wi-Fi, switch to **Mode B or C** to generate your flashcards, quiz, and AI tutor outputs at the highest quality. You get the privacy benefit of on-device recording during class and the accuracy benefit of cloud processing when it matters for studying.
</Tip>

The Education plan defaults to **Mode A/B** — on-device and hybrid processing — with a small cloud allowance included. This keeps the plan sustainable for high-frequency student use. You won't burn through your cloud allowance just by recording lectures; the heavy cloud processing is reserved for generating study outputs when you choose to trigger them.

## Summer Pause: Your Data Waits for You

At the end of each semester, your Education plan **auto-pauses**. You are not charged during the summer break. All your course data, lecture recordings, flashcards, and course memory are **retained** — nothing is deleted. When your next term starts, resume your plan and pick up exactly where you left off.

## The Lecturer Plan

If you are a verified lecturer at a university, BRIEF is **free** for you. The lecturer plan gives you access to BRIEF's lecture recording and output tools so you can create structured study materials directly from your own lectures. You can share those materials — summaries, concept lists, flashcard decks — with your students, giving them a BRIEF-generated foundation for revision before they even open the app themselves.

To apply for the lecturer plan, sign up with your **verified institutional email address** and select the lecturer option during onboarding. BRIEF's team will verify your status and activate your free access.

## The AI Tutor: Source-Traceable Study Artifacts

Most AI study tools generate flashcards and quiz questions from general knowledge — the AI writes a question about "Newton's laws" from what it already knows, not from what your lecturer actually said. BRIEF works differently.

Every flashcard, quiz question, and tutor answer in BRIEF is **grounded in your transcript**. When you tap a flashcard, BRIEF shows you the exact moment in the lecture recording it came from. When the AI tutor explains a concept, it is drawing on *your lecturer's words*, in *your lecturer's framing*, from *your specific session*.

This matters because:
- Your exam is on **what your lecturer taught**, not on the canonical internet explanation.
- You can verify any answer by jumping to the source timestamp.
- Nothing is fabricated — if BRIEF can't ground an answer in your recording, it tells you.

## How to Sign Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download BRIEF">
    Get BRIEF from the App Store or Google Play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'I'm a student' at signup">
    On the onboarding screen, select the student path. This routes you to Education plan pricing and verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your university email or student ID">
    Enter your university email address. If your institution's email domain is recognized, verification is instant. Otherwise, you can submit a student ID for manual review. You must be 18 or older to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your semester">
    Select your current semester start date. BRIEF will set your plan duration and auto-pause date based on a standard 4-month semester.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    You're ready. Open a new conversation, select the **Lecture** type, and start recording. BRIEF handles the rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I'm 18 but not at a university yet. Can I use the Education plan?">
    No. The Education plan requires active enrollment at a university or higher-education institution. Gap year students or recent graduates are not eligible — you'll need a standard plan until you're enrolled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my university email doesn't work for verification?">
    You can submit a student ID or enrollment letter for manual verification. The BRIEF team reviews these within one business day. Use any official document that shows your name, institution, and current enrollment status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the summer pause happen automatically?">
    Yes. Your plan auto-pauses at the end of the semester period you selected at signup. You don't need to do anything. You'll receive a notification before the pause takes effect, and another when your resumption window opens at the start of the next term.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use BRIEF for non-lecture recordings on the Education plan?">
    The Education plan includes the full suite of lecture-type outputs. You can also use standard conversation types available to your plan tier for other recordings — study group sessions, office hours, or personal voice memos — within your cloud allowance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the lecturer plan really free?">
    Yes. Verified lecturers at accredited universities get free access to BRIEF's lecture recording and output tools. The plan is free as long as your institutional affiliation remains active and verified.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
