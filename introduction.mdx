BRIEF turns your spoken conversations into structured, searchable memory. Whether you're closing a deal, running a project meeting, or interviewing a research subject, BRIEF captures what was said, identifies who said it, extracts what matters — decisions, actions, risks, commitments — and stores it in a searchable archive you can return to any time.

## What BRIEF Does

Every conversation BRIEF processes moves through three stages:

- **Capture** — Record a conversation with one tap. BRIEF works offline, so you never miss a moment waiting for a signal.
- **Understand** — BRIEF transcribes speech in Arabic, English, or both in the same sentence, identifies each speaker, classifies the conversation type, and extracts structured outputs automatically.
- **Remember** — Every result is saved to your Conversation Archive with full provenance: every decision and action item links back to the exact moment in the transcript it was spoken.

## Who BRIEF Is For

BRIEF is built for professionals whose work depends on remembering what was said and agreed:

- **Consultants and freelancers** who need a clear record of client briefs and commitments
- **Agency owners and managers** who run multiple meetings a day and can't afford gaps in follow-through
- **Project managers** who need decisions and action items captured with owners and deadlines
- **Sales professionals** who must track client requirements, objections, and next steps
- **Researchers and analysts** who conduct interviews and need to search across conversations

If you work in Arabic, switch between Arabic and English in the same meeting, or simply need an honest record of what was agreed — BRIEF was made for you.

## Two Conversation Experiences

BRIEF automatically classifies every conversation into one of two types based on what it hears — you don't need to choose before you record.

**Professional Conversation** is designed for structured business contexts: client meetings, project kick-offs, stand-ups, negotiations, and interviews. It produces:

- Summary and key points
- Decisions made
- Action items with owners and deadlines
- Risks identified
- Requirements captured
- Commitments given and received
- Follow-up items

**Generic Conversation** is designed for open-ended discussions, brainstorms, and conversations that don't follow a formal agenda. It produces:

- Summary and key points
- Questions raised
- Action items
- Important moments

The classification appears as an editable chip on your output — if BRIEF gets it wrong, you can switch the type with one tap and the outputs regenerate.

## Your Conversation Archive

Every processed conversation is automatically saved to your Conversation Archive. The archive is searchable across titles, transcripts, and extracted fields. Every fact BRIEF surfaces — every decision, every action item, every commitment — includes a provenance link that jumps you back to the exact timestamp in the original transcript where it was said.
