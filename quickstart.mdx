---
title: "Quickstart: Record Your First Conversation with BRIEF"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Install BRIEF, record a conversation, and get structured decisions, actions, and a summary — no account required for your first recording."
---

Get from install to structured output in under five minutes. This guide walks you through your first recording end-to-end: installing the app, capturing a conversation, reviewing the output, and saving it to your archive. You don't need an account to complete any of these steps.

<Note>
  No account is required to record, process on-device, and view your results. Create an account only if you want cloud-enhanced processing (Mode C), sync across devices, or multi-device memory.
</Note>

<Steps>

  <Step title="Install BRIEF">
    Download BRIEF from the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android) and open the app.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Download for iOS" icon="apple" href="https://apps.apple.com">
        Requires iOS 16 or later.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Download for Android" icon="google-play" href="https://play.google.com">
        Requires Android 10 or later.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    When you open BRIEF for the first time, grant microphone permission when prompted. BRIEF cannot record without it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap Record">
    On the home screen, tap the **Record** button. BRIEF opens the recording setup screen.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="First-time users">
        Choose your **processing mode** — this controls where your audio is handled. Each option is described briefly on screen. Pick the one that fits your situation:

        | Mode | What happens to your audio | Best for |
        |------|---------------------------|----------|
        | **A — On-Device** | Audio never leaves your phone | Maximum privacy, works fully offline |
        | **B — Hybrid** | Audio stays local; optional text processing uses the cloud | Balance of privacy and capability |
        | **C — Cloud-Enhanced** | Audio is sent to the cloud for processing, then deleted | Best transcription accuracy |

        <Tip>
          If this is your first recording, start with **Mode A** to get a feel for BRIEF with no data leaving your device. You can switch modes at any time — your choice is saved as the default for future recordings and can be changed on the setup screen before each session.
        </Tip>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Repeat users">
        The setup screen pre-fills your last-used processing mode. Confirm the mode or change it, then tap **Start Recording**.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record Your Conversation">
    Once you tap **Start Recording**, BRIEF begins capturing audio. A **visible recording indicator** stays on-screen at all times — BRIEF never records silently or in the background without your knowledge.

    While recording, you can:

    - **Highlight a moment** — Tap the ★ star (bookmark) icon to flag an important moment in real time. BRIEF marks that timestamp so it surfaces prominently in your output.
    - **Pause and resume** — Use the pause button if you need a break without ending the session.

    <Warning>
      Do not close the app while recording. If the app is interrupted unexpectedly, BRIEF uses chunked storage to recover everything captured up to that point — but closing the app manually ends the session.
    </Warning>

    Tap **Stop** when the conversation is finished.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for Processing">
    BRIEF automatically begins processing the moment you tap Stop. Depending on your chosen mode and conversation length, this takes seconds to a few minutes. During processing, BRIEF:

    1. Transcribes speech in Arabic, English, or code-switched Arabic/English
    2. Identifies and labels each speaker (Speaker 1, Speaker 2, etc.)
    3. Classifies the conversation as **Professional** or **Generic**
    4. Extracts structured outputs based on the conversation type

    You'll see a progress indicator on screen. You can leave the app open or return to the home screen — BRIEF notifies you when processing is complete.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Your Output">
    Open the completed conversation to see your structured output. The layout depends on how BRIEF classified the conversation.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Professional Conversation">
        BRIEF surfaces the following sections:

        - **Summary** — A concise overview of the conversation
        - **Key Points** — The most important statements
        - **Decisions** — What was agreed or resolved
        - **Action Items** — Tasks, with the owner and deadline inferred from context
        - **Risks** — Issues or concerns flagged during the conversation
        - **Requirements** — Specifications or constraints captured
        - **Commitments** — Promises made by any participant
        - **Follow-ups** — Items that need attention after the meeting
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Generic Conversation">
        BRIEF surfaces the following sections:

        - **Summary** — A concise overview of the conversation
        - **Key Points** — The most important statements
        - **Questions** — Open questions raised during the conversation
        - **Action Items** — Tasks identified
        - **Important Moments** — Bookmarked highlights and other notable points
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    <Note>
      The conversation type appears as a chip at the top of the output (e.g., **Professional** or **Generic**). If BRIEF classified it incorrectly, tap the chip to switch types — the output regenerates immediately.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Correct Speaker Names">
    BRIEF labels speakers as **Speaker 1**, **Speaker 2**, and so on. Based on context, it also infers likely names and displays them as suggestions.

    To confirm or correct a speaker name:

    1. Tap any speaker label in the transcript or output
    2. Type the correct name or confirm the suggested one
    3. Tap **Save** — the name updates everywhere in the conversation

    <Tip>
      Correcting speaker names once makes your archived conversations significantly easier to search later, especially if you meet the same people regularly.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share or Export">
    Tap the **Share** icon to send your output in the format that works for the recipient.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="WhatsApp" icon="whatsapp">
        Send a formatted summary directly to a contact or group.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Copy to Clipboard" icon="clipboard">
        Paste the output into any app — email, notes, or a project management tool.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Export as PDF" icon="file-pdf">
        Generate a formatted PDF document, ideal for formal records or client handoffs.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Export as DOCX" icon="file-word">
        Export an editable Word document for further editing or filing.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    Outputs are available in **Arabic** and **English** — use the language toggle on the share screen to choose the format you need.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find It in Your Archive">
    Your conversation is automatically saved to the **Conversation Archive**. To access it:

    1. Tap **Archive** in the bottom navigation
    2. Use the search bar to find conversations by title, keyword, speaker name, or extracted content
    3. Tap any conversation to reopen the full output and transcript

    Every decision, action item, and commitment in the archive includes a **provenance link** — tap it to jump to the exact moment in the transcript where it was said.
  </Step>

</Steps>
