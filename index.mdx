## What BRIEF does for you

BRIEF follows a simple three-step loop every time you record:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture">
    Tap record on your phone. BRIEF works offline — no internet required. Recording is always visible and consent-first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understand">
    BRIEF transcribes in Arabic, English, and mixed Arabic/English. It identifies who spoke, determines what kind of conversation it was, and extracts the information that matters: decisions, action items, requirements, and commitments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remember">
    Every conversation is stored in your personal Conversation Archive — searchable, re-readable, and provenance-linked so you can always trace a fact back to the exact moment it was said.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Privacy built in, not bolted on

Your audio can stay entirely on your device. BRIEF gives you three processing modes — fully on-device, hybrid, and cloud-enhanced — and you choose which one fits your situation. The Privacy Center lets you see exactly what was processed and where, and delete any conversation completely.

<Note>
  BRIEF never records in the background, never listens silently, and never trains on your conversations without your explicit consent. Every recording starts with a tap and is visible on screen while it runs.
</Note>
