Independent consulting and freelance work runs on verbal agreements — and verbal agreements are where scope creep, missed requirements, and billing disputes are born. BRIEF captures every client meeting the moment it happens, extracts requirements and commitments in the exact words used, and produces a محضر your client recognizes as professional documentation — all without you touching a notepad.

## The Problem BRIEF Solves

Client requirements arrive in a mix of Egyptian Arabic, Gulf dialect, and English technical terms. They change between the first meeting and the kickoff call. By the time a disagreement surfaces, the client remembers something different from what you remember — and neither of you has a record.

## Core Workflow

Follow this sequence at every client meeting to build a watertight record from day one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Up the Recording">
    Open BRIEF before the meeting starts. Enter the client name and project in the setup screen — this is what makes the Conversation Archive searchable later. Select your processing mode (see Privacy below) and tap **Record**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Have the Meeting Normally">
    Speak naturally in whatever mix of Arabic and English the conversation calls for. BRIEF is actively recording — the screen shows the recording state at all times. You do not need to announce topics or speak slowly — BRIEF handles natural conversation flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop and Review">
    When the meeting ends, tap **Stop**. BRIEF generates the structured output — requirements, commitments, decisions, action items, risks, and a full محضر — in under two minutes on Mode C, or immediately on Mode A (on-device).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the محضر Before You Leave">
    Tap **Share via WhatsApp** and send the formatted محضر to the client's number or group. Do this before you leave the room. The client receives a professional Arabic summary of everything agreed while the conversation is still fresh for both of you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive and Move On">
    The conversation is automatically saved to your BRIEF archive under the client and project name you entered. You're done. No transcription, no manual notes, no follow-up email to write from memory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What BRIEF Extracts

BRIEF's Professional Conversation output identifies the following from your client meeting:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Requirements">
    Every stated requirement is surfaced as a named section in the output. This becomes your scope confirmation — forward it back to the client as a written record of what was requested. When scope changes later, you have the original.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Commitments">
    Commitments made by both parties are extracted and linked to the timestamp in the recording where they were spoken. 'You agreed to include X' becomes 'you said on [date] at 14:32: [exact quote]'. Disputes are resolved before they escalate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Action Items">
    Each action item is extracted with an owner and, where stated, a deadline. Action items assigned to you are separated from those assigned to the client.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Decisions">
    Key decisions are captured with the context around them — who decided, and what was said when the decision was made.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Risks and Follow-Ups">
    Anything flagged as uncertain, pending approval, or subject to change is surfaced under Risks. Open questions and agreed follow-up points appear in a dedicated Follow-Ups section.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Searching Past Client Conversations

Over time, your BRIEF archive becomes a complete record of every client engagement.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Search by Client">
    Type the client name in the Conversation Archive search bar. Every recorded meeting with that client appears in chronological order — requirements from the first call, commitments from the kickoff, decisions from mid-project reviews.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Search by Keyword">
    Search 'budget' or 'deadline' or any technical term to surface every moment across all recordings where that word appeared. Useful when you remember something was said but not when or by whom.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Jump to the Moment">
    Tap any extracted fact — a requirement, a commitment, a decision — and BRIEF jumps to the exact timestamp in the recording where it was said. You hear the original words in context.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Privacy and Processing Modes

<Note>
  BRIEF offers three processing modes. Choose based on your location and the sensitivity of the conversation.
</Note>

| Mode | Processing | Best For |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **A — On-Device** | All processing happens on your phone. Audio never leaves the device. | Sensitive client conversations, café meetings, no-signal environments |
| **B — Hybrid** | Anonymised processing split between device and cloud. | Most professional meetings |
| **C — Cloud** | Full cloud processing. Fastest results, highest accuracy. | Post-meeting processing when back at the office |

<Tip>
  Use Mode A if you're in a public space like a café or co-working space with no signal. The محضر is generated entirely on-device — there is no connectivity requirement.
</Tip>

## The محضر as a Client Deliverable

The BRIEF محضر is formatted for professional Arabic business communication. Many consultants and freelancers send it to clients without editing a single word — the structure, the language register, and the level of detail are calibrated for professional use. Clients who receive it treat it as the official record of the meeting.

<Tip>
  Sending the محضر immediately — before you've left the room or the parking lot — is the habit that changes the relationship. It signals professionalism, creates a shared record, and gives the client no room to later say 'that wasn't what we agreed'.
</Tip>

## Tips for Consultants

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Name the client during setup — every time">
    BRIEF works best when you name the client or project during the recording setup. This makes the Conversation Archive much easier to search later. A recording labeled 'Shereif / Brand Refresh Q3' is instantly findable six months from now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use requirements extraction as a scope confirmation email">
    After sending the WhatsApp محضر, copy the Requirements section into an email with the subject 'Scope Confirmation — [Project Name]'. Ask the client to confirm. This two-step process creates both an immediate record (WhatsApp) and a formal one (email reply chain).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Record project update calls, not just kickoffs">
    Requirements change most dangerously in mid-project update calls, when everyone is moving fast and assumes the other person is noting everything. Record every client call, not just the first one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Offline first, always">
    Set BRIEF to Mode A by default and switch to Mode C only when you need the extra speed. This way, you never have to think about connectivity — the recording always works.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
