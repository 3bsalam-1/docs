Every conversation you record in BRIEF becomes a permanent, searchable entry in your Conversation Archive — BRIEF's AI Memory layer. Rather than leaving you with a folder of audio files you'll never revisit, the Archive stores structured knowledge: decisions made, actions assigned, commitments given, and the exact moments where each of those things was said. Over time, your Archive becomes the most complete record of your professional life that you actually own.

## What the Conversation Archive Is

The Conversation Archive is a searchable, provenance-linked record of every conversation you have recorded. It is personal to you, stored under your account, and governed by the processing mode and retention settings you have chosen. Think of it as a long-term memory that is always available, always accurate to what was actually said, and always traceable back to the source.

<Tip>
  The Conversation Archive builds value over time. The more conversations you record, the richer and more useful your searchable history becomes — past decisions surface when you need them, and commitments never quietly disappear.
</Tip>

## What Is Stored for Each Conversation

Every entry in your Archive contains a consistent set of fields, regardless of conversation type or processing mode.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Identity and metadata">
    <ResponseField name="Conversation ID" type="string">
      A unique identifier assigned at the moment recording stops. Used to anchor all provenance links and to connect any future cross-conversation references back to the right recording.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Date and time" type="timestamp">
      The date and local time the recording started, derived from your device clock.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Title" type="string">
      Auto-generated by BRIEF from the summary and key points immediately after processing. You can edit the title at any time — tap it on the conversation detail screen to rename it.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Conversation type" type="enum">
      Either **Professional** or **Generic**, as classified automatically by BRIEF (or as corrected by you). Stored alongside the output it produced.
    </ResponseField>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transcript and audio reference">
    <ResponseField name="Transcript" type="text">
      The full, speaker-labelled transcript of the conversation, including any speaker names inferred or corrected after processing.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Audio reference" type="reference">
      A pointer to the audio file, subject to your retention policy. Depending on your settings, audio may be retained indefinitely, for a set number of days, or deleted immediately after processing. The transcript and structured output remain in the Archive regardless of audio retention.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Important timestamps" type="array">
      A set of automatically identified moments in the recording that correspond to extracted items — decisions, action items, commitments, and important moments. Used to power the jump-to-moment feature.
    </ResponseField>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Structured output">
    <ResponseField name="Summary" type="text">
      The narrative summary generated for the conversation.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Extracted fields" type="object">
      All structured fields produced for the conversation type: decisions, action items (with owners and deadlines), risks, requirements, commitments, follow-ups (Professional) or key points, questions, and important moments (Generic).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Speaker labels and names" type="array">
      The plain-text speaker labels and any inferred or corrected names, stored as text — never as biometric data.
    </ResponseField>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Provenance and policy metadata">
    <ResponseField name="Provenance links" type="array">
      For every extracted item, a link to the exact timestamp in the transcript where it was spoken.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Processing mode" type="enum">
      The mode used to process this conversation: **A** (On This Phone Only), **B** (Hybrid), or **C** (Cloud Enhanced). Stored permanently so you always know how a given conversation was handled.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Consent record" type="object">
      A record of what disclosure was made before or during recording, when it was made, and which Recording Policy applied. See [Privacy & Consent](/concepts/privacy-consent) for details.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="Tags" type="array">
      Basic tags you apply manually, or that BRIEF suggests, to help you organise and filter the Archive.
    </ResponseField>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What You Can Do With the Archive

### Browse your history

Open the Archive from the main navigation. Conversations appear newest first. Each card shows the title, date, conversation type, and a one-line summary. Scroll to explore; use filters to narrow down.

### Filter by type, tag, or date

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Filter by type" icon="tag">
    Show only Professional or only Generic conversations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Filter by tag" icon="hashtag">
    Show only conversations with a specific tag you've applied.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Filter by date" icon="calendar">
    Narrow to a date range — useful when you remember roughly when a conversation happened.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Search across your entire Archive

Type any word or phrase into the search bar. BRIEF searches across:

- Conversation **titles**
- Full **transcripts**
- **Summaries**
- All **extracted fields** — decisions, action items, requirements, commitments, and the rest

Results show the conversation they came from and the specific field or transcript line where your query matched. Tap a result to open the full conversation at that point.

### Search within a conversation

Open any conversation and use the in-conversation search bar to find a specific word, name, or phrase within that recording's transcript and output. Every match is highlighted and linked to its timestamp.

### Retrieve a fact and jump to the moment it was said

Every extracted item — every decision, action item, commitment, or important moment — carries a timestamp link. Tap the link icon next to any item to jump to the exact moment in the recording where it was spoken.

<Note>
  Jumping to the audio moment requires that audio is still retained under your retention policy. If audio has been deleted, the transcript segment is shown instead.
</Note>

## The Provenance Principle

BRIEF is built around a single principle: every piece of structured output must be traceable back to the words that produced it. This is not just a feature — it is an architectural constraint applied across the entire extraction pipeline.

When BRIEF surfaces a decision, you can see the transcript line it came from. When it assigns an action item to a person, you can hear the sentence where their name and the task appeared together. When it identifies a commitment, you can verify that someone actually said what BRIEF claims they said.

<Warning>
  If an extracted item looks wrong, tap its provenance link before editing or dismissing it. Sometimes the AI extraction is correct but the speaker label needs correcting — fixing the label resolves the apparent error without losing the extracted item.
</Warning>

## Memory Evolution

The Conversation Archive is the foundation of BRIEF's AI Memory layer. It is fully functional today. Future versions of BRIEF will build on this foundation to add:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cross-conversation AI chat" icon="messages">
    Ask questions across your entire Archive — "What did we decide about the pricing model?" — and get answers with provenance links to the conversations where those decisions were made.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Entity resolution" icon="link">
    Automatically link the same person, project, or organisation across many conversations — so you can see every decision involving a client, or every action item assigned to a colleague, across your full history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team memory" icon="users">
    Share structured outputs and a searchable collective archive across a team, with individual consent and permission controls for every participant.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Longitudinal insights" icon="chart-line">
    Surface patterns over time — recurring blockers, unresolved follow-ups, commitments that were never actioned — drawn from months of conversation data.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The data model and provenance architecture that BRIEF uses today are designed to support all of these capabilities. Every conversation you record now is an investment in a more powerful memory layer as BRIEF evolves.
